THE TESTING PROTOCOL

How We Work

A structured 5-stage engineering sequence ensuring zero discrepancy between design intent and field operations.

01

Inspection & Plan Review

Reviewing MEP schematics, submittals, and system design criteria prior to site mobilization.

02

Instrumentation & Testing

Setting up traceably calibrated flow hoods, orifice test plates, micro-manometers, and Pitot tubes.

03

System Air & Water Balancing

Adjusting balancing dampers, hydronic valves, fan speeds, and pump pressures to target GPM/CFM.

04

Verification & Compliance Audit

Performing final verification tests against SMACNA, ASHRAE, and NEBB code standards.

05

Final Certified Report Handover

Compiling empirical test reports, SMACNA leakage certifications, and commissioning documentation.

ENGINEERING FAQ

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Answers to common MEP consultant and facility manager inquiries regarding TAB and duct pressure testing.

Duct pressure testing involves sealing a duct section, connecting a calibrated orifice test rig with micro-manometers, pressurizing to design static pressure (e.g. 2", 4", or 10" w.g.), and measuring leakage CFM against SMACNA Leakage Class standards.

TAB ensures that design airflows (CFM) and water flows (GPM) match actual field values. Without balancing, commercial buildings suffer hot/cold spots, excessive chiller power consumption, noise issues, and air imbalance.
