FULL CAPABILITY SPECTRUM

Commercial HVAC Engineering Divisions

Empirical airflow, pressure, smoke, water flow, optical pass, and protective coating solutions.

Air Balancing (TAB)

Adjusting supply, return, and exhaust diffusers using digital capture hoods to achieve exact design volumetric airflow (CFM) across all floors.

  • Direct CFM capture hood & Pitot traverse readings
  • AHU & FCU fan rpm and motor amperage balancing
  • Floor-by-floor differential pressure cascades

SMACNA Duct Pressure Test

Pressurizing sealed duct sections using calibrated orifice test rigs to measure static pressure loss and verify zero leakage compliance.

  • SMACNA Leakage Class 2, 4, 6 & 12 verification
  • High/Medium/Low static pressure duct testing
  • Digital micro-manometer orifice plate logging

Smoke & Containment Testing

Visualizing micro-leaks, room air currents, and negative/positive isolation pressure boundaries using non-toxic dense tracer smoke.

  • Hospital operating room pressure containment
  • Pharma cleanroom HEPA airflow pattern audit
  • Smoke evacuation damper operational check

Water Balancing (TAB)

Measuring and balancing chilled water flow rates (GPM) across cooling coils, pumps, and chillers using ultrasonic non-invasive flow meters.

  • Ultrasonic non-invasive transit-time GPM logging
  • Hydronic balancing valve setting adjustment
  • Chiller plant Delta-T thermal efficiency optimization

Optical Light Pass Testing

Scanning internal duct seams with high-candela optical light emitters to pinpoint unsealed transverse joints before insulation wrapping.

  • High-intensity light pass-through beam audit
  • Rapid pre-insulation joint seam inspection
  • Visual defect mapping for MEP contractors

Duct Painting & Acoustic Coating

Applying specialized anti-corrosive paints, acoustic damping coats, and architectural finishes to exposed ceiling ductwork.

  • Anti-corrosion galvanized duct coating
  • Acoustic vibration dampening paint layer
  • Architectural black/grey exposed duct finish
